Practice

How we work.

Four habits that decide whether an app survives its first year.

Ownership
We don't take clients. Every app here is ours — we carry the cost, the risk and the consequences, which is the only arrangement that keeps you honest about what actually works.
Transformation
A raw idea isn't a product. It gets forged: strategy, design, code, then relentless iteration against what the numbers say rather than what we hoped they'd say.
Data-driven
Retention cohorts, acquisition channels, monetisation curves. Taste sets the direction; data settles the argument. We instrument everything before we defend anything.
Innovation
The mobile landscape resets every eighteen months. We stay ahead on acquisition, monetisation and creative so our apps don't quietly age out of their category.

Company

Named after a furnace.

The athanor is the alchemist's oven: a vessel built to hold one steady temperature for months, long after the fire stops being interesting. Sealed inside it sits the philosophical egg — the thing being slowly transformed. That's the mark at the top of this page.

It's also the part of app-building nobody photographs. Not the launch, but the two hundred quiet weeks afterwards: the pricing test that finally lands, the onboarding rewritten for the fourth time, the retention curve that bends a little. Athanor Studio was founded in 2024 on the belief that any idea, given the right process and enough time at temperature, can become extraordinary.
